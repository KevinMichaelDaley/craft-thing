# Restore exact sparse gas publication and verify the omitted first-axis copies

Two targeted gas publication checks fail on the exact c4324106 predecessor and on the sparse-advection correction. The direct-first-axis comparison reports 57344000 bytes of GPU copies on both routes with identical snapshots. The bank-owner comparison reports 53 dispatches each but different assembled snapshots. The separate pressure-noise admission test passes.

## Acceptance Criteria

Identify why the fixture retains the full copy or diverges under sparse ownership, then restore fewer copies and bit-identical stock and state at mapped and dormant owners through policy changes. Keep the existing assertions and verify the Vulkan publication suite.
