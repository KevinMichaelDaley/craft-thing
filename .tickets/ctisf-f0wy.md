# Validate the canonical world pages across an ice storm footprint

The storm resolves only the center by world ownership and then paints and cools physical neighbors. A remapped or stale adjacent page can belong to an unrelated signed world chunk.

## Acceptance Criteria

Reject mismatched addressed pages before any water or heat change. A mapped negative seam still receives the exact disc, genuinely absent pages remain untouched, and a page outside the circle does not block the cast. Cover the production host path and a real Vulkan remapped neighbor.
