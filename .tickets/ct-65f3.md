# Keep grown runtime geometry in mapped VRAM after growth

Half-native NVIDIA A2000 trace: pixel appearance grows to 68 MiB and metadata to 23.4 MiB while old local buffers remain allocated. Both new buffers fall back to system memory even though the aperture has room once old generations are freed. Rehome completed replacement buffers to mapped VRAM after retiring old buffers, preserving geometry, addresses, descriptors and no allocation failure regression.

## Acceptance Criteria

Displayed half-native growth places 68 MiB appearance buffer in mapped local VRAM; focused geometry growth tests and full suite pass; normal-clock half-native 32-frame benchmark improves mean/worst; no validation errors.
